01 · THE IDEA
A tool that recedes.

Most productivity apps fight for your attention with streaks, badges, and a buzzing inbox. Brain.exe does the opposite. It hands you your priorities, the time to do them, then steps out of the way.

Tasks, calendar, notes, and a focus timer share one quiet surface. Open it in the morning and the day already makes sense. No setup, no onboarding maze, nothing competing for the part of your mind you'd rather spend on the work itself.

02 · THE INTELLIGENCE

It thinks a step ahead.

Brain.exe reads the shape of your day: what's urgent, what's overdue, what unblocks the most. Then it quietly names the single thing worth doing next. Not a chatbot. A sense of direction.
